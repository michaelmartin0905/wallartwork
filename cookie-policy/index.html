Q.01What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file a website can place in your browser to remember something — for example that you are signed in, or which pages you have viewed. Related technologies such as local storage, tracking pixels and device fingerprinting can do similar jobs.

Q.02Does wallartwork.it.com set cookies?

No. The code that makes up wallartwork.it.com does not create cookies, and the site does not use local storage, tracking pixels or fingerprinting. There are no analytics tools, advertising networks, social media plug-ins or embedded third-party widgets.

The only exception is at the server level: our hosting infrastructure may set a strictly necessary technical cookie, for example to protect the site against abuse or to balance traffic. Such cookies do not identify you personally and are not used for marketing.

Q.03What about the fonts on this site?

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser requests the font files directly from Google’s servers, which receive your IP address and browser information as part of that request. The font service is designed to work without setting cookies on our pages. More detail on how we treat information is in our Privacy Policy.

Q.05Do email links use cookies?

No. Clicking an email address on this site simply opens the email application on your device. Anything you then send is covered by our Privacy Policy.

Q.06How can I control cookies?

Every major browser lets you view, block and delete cookies. Menu names change between versions, but you will usually find the controls here:

  • Chrome — Settings, then Privacy and security, then Third-party cookies or Site data.
  • Firefox — Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Cookies and Site Data.
  • Safari — Settings or Preferences, then Privacy, then Manage Website Data.
  • Edge — Settings, then Cookies and site permissions.
  • Mobile browsers — the browser’s own settings menu, usually under Privacy or Site settings.

Blocking cookies will not stop wallartwork.it.com from working, because the site does not depend on them.

Q.07Why is there no cookie banner?

Consent banners exist to ask permission for non-essential cookies. Because this website doesn’t set preference, analytics or advertising cookies, there is nothing optional to ask about. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated and consent will be requested where required.
