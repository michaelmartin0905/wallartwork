At a glance

  • No contact forms, accounts, analytics or advertising trackers on this website.
  • We receive personal information mainly when you choose to email us.
  • We never sell personal information.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time.

About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how wallartwork.it.com (“we”, “us”, “our”) treats personal information connected with this website and with conversations about our wall art services. It covers information you send us directly and information that is recorded technically when you browse the site.

The website is informational. It has no user accounts, no online checkout and no enquiry forms. Contact happens by email, which you choose to start.

Information you choose to send us

When you email support@wallartwork.it.com, we receive whatever you include. Typically that is:

  • your name and email address, and any phone number or company name you add;
  • details about your space, such as its location, use and timing;
  • photographs, measurements, sketches and reference images of walls or artwork;
  • anything else you decide to share in the message or attachments.

Please avoid sending sensitive information (for example health or financial details) that isn’t needed to discuss a wall art project. If photographs show other people, please make sure you are comfortable sharing them.

What is recorded when you browse

Like most websites, the server that hosts wallartwork.it.com may automatically keep standard access logs. These logs can include:

  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • browser type, device type and operating system;
  • the pages and files requested, with date and time;
  • the referring web address, if your browser sends one.

We do not run analytics, advertising or tracking scripts on this website, and we do not build profiles of visitors. Our approach to cookies is set out separately in our Cookie Policy.

Fonts delivered by a third party

To display its typefaces, this website asks Google Fonts to deliver font files to your browser. When that happens, your browser connects to Google’s servers, which receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details. Google handles that information under its own privacy terms. If you prefer, you can block third-party font requests in your browser; the site will fall back to system fonts.

How we use information

To reply and plan

We use the details in your email to answer questions, understand your wall and prepare any proposal you ask for.

To deliver agreed work

If a project goes ahead, we use the information needed to schedule, carry out and complete it.

To keep the site working

Server logs may be used to keep the website secure, diagnose faults and prevent misuse.

To meet obligations

We may keep certain records where required for legal, tax or accounting reasons.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for automated decision-making.

When information is shared

We share personal information only where it is needed:

  • Service providers who host the website or provide our email service, acting on our behalf;
  • Professional advisers such as accountants or legal advisers, where reasonably necessary;
  • Authorities where the law requires us to disclose information or to protect rights and safety;
  • A successor if the business or website is transferred, in which case this policy would continue to protect your information.

Photographs of your space are not published without your permission.

How long information is kept

Email correspondence is kept for as long as it is useful for the conversation or project it relates to, and afterwards for as long as reasonably needed for records, follow-up questions or legal reasons. Server logs are kept for a limited period set by the hosting provider. When information is no longer needed, it is deleted or anonymised.

Keeping information secure

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including secure (HTTPS) delivery of this website. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security, but we work to protect what you share with us.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and request a copy;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate information;
  • ask us to delete information we no longer need;
  • object to or limit certain uses of your information.

To make a request, email support@wallartwork.it.com. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on it. You will not be treated differently for exercising your rights.

Children

This website and our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Other websites

If this site links to another website, that website’s own privacy practices apply. We are not responsible for how other websites handle personal information.

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy as the website or our services change. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page.
