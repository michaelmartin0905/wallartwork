Helpful details

What to include in your email.

You don’t need a finished brief. These details simply help us understand your wall and reply with useful next steps.

  1. The space

    Home, restaurant, office, shop or exterior — and what the room is used for.

  2. The wall

    A straight-on photo and approximate width and height.

  3. The surface

    Brick, plaster, drywall, concrete or texture, plus any known damp or cracks.

  4. The idea

    References, colours, words or a mood. A loose description is fine.

  5. The service

    Which wall art service interests you, if you already know.

  6. Timing & location

    Where the wall is and any dates or opening hours to work around.
